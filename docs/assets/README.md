- `saved-comparison.png` and `saved-comparison-mobile.png`: Chromium screenshots of the saved-policy comparison, persistent original market, market seed 43000011 and SAC seed 1. Single-market illustration; the table includes the saved multi-seed averages. [Browser checks and image hashes](saved-comparison-validation.json).

- `control-comparison.png` and `control-comparison.json`: application overview generated by [`scripts/plot_application.py`](../../scripts/plot_application.py) from the original-market main study's saved paired differences and intervals. All six scenarios and both pure-SAC training conditions are included. Metadata records input/source/figure hashes and all 24 plotted estimates/intervals; no new evaluation or training was performed. The original point-estimate figure remains in the result record.
- `demo.png`: real desktop Chromium screenshot of the default balanced-reward demo (100 episodes × 100 steps, 200 players, seed 0, fixed 5% fee). These are training charts, separate from the frozen-policy evaluation. [Exact settings and browser checks](demo-validation.json).
- `demo-mobile.png`: the same completed run at a 390px viewport.
- `original-deployment.png`: original poster embedded image 004, extracted directly with `pdfimages -png`.

The unused `original-environment.png` extraction was removed during submission
cleanup; its source diagram remains in the original PDF. The deployment diagram
above is used by the game-integration documentation.

Historical comparison figures remain in the [original poster](../../Poster/capstone-poster.pdf); extracted copies and the earlier demo curve are in the evidence archive. Current experimental figures have separate provenance under [`results/reevaluation/`](../../results/reevaluation/).
